      Pack the node chunk

      AiSAQ pays for its tiny DRAM footprint by duplicating neighbor PQ codes on SSD. The practical question is whether those extra bytes cross another 4 KB block boundary.

      “DRAM-free” has an asterisk

      The strong claim is dataset-size-independent PQ working memory—not literal zero RAM and not free storage.

      What gets lighter

      • Measured process memory: 11–14 MB
      • Index load time: 0.6–2.0 ms in the single-server tests
      • Switching between indices that share PQ centroids

      What gets heavier

      • Neighbor PQ codes are duplicated across graph edges on SSD
      • Larger node chunks may require more 4 KB reads
      • The evaluated implementation lacks filtering and dynamic indexing
